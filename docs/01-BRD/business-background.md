## Business Background

The organization operates a traditional family-managed chit collection business.

Multiple chit batches run simultaneously throughout the year.

Each batch consists of members subscribing to different fixed-value plans such as:

• ₹50,000

• ₹1,00,000

• ₹2,00,000

• ₹5,00,000

Every plan follows a predefined 20-installment schedule and a predefined withdrawal schedule.

Members may enroll in multiple plans within the same batch or across different batches.

The organizer manually maintains payment records, installment status, withdrawal details, and member documents using notebooks and paper files.

The business currently depends on manual calculations and physical document storage.
