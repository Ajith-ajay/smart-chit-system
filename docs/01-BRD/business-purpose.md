## Purpose

The purpose of this project is to digitize and streamline the day-to-day operations of a family-operated chit management business.

The current business relies heavily on handwritten notebooks, manual payment tracking, physical receipts, and paper-based declarations. As the number of members and concurrent chit batches has grown, maintaining accurate records has become increasingly difficult and time-consuming.

The proposed Smart Chit Management System (SCMS) will replace these manual processes with a secure, centralized, cloud-based application. The system will enable authorized family members to manage member registrations, batch enrollments, installment collections, withdrawal processing, document storage, and reporting from a single platform.

The primary objective is to improve operational efficiency, reduce manual errors, maintain complete financial records, and provide a scalable foundation for future business growth.
